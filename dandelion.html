Dandelion Yarns

Founder & Creative

Dandelion Yarns was my own hand-dyed yarn brand, where I developed everything from color palettes and collections to visual identity and communication. The work was rooted in a hands-on, material-driven process, where dyeing, texture, and color were central to each collection.

Alongside the physical making, I built a visual world around the brand through photography and storytelling, primarily on Instagram. Over time, it grew into an engaged community, where atmosphere, color, and a strong sense of identity shaped how the work was experienced.
