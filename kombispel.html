Work

Kombispel
(2014 – 2016)

Graphic Designer & Art Director

At Kombispel, I worked with graphic design and art direction across a range of projects, from campaigns and print material to digital communication. The work involved developing visual concepts as well as refining them into clear and consistent design across different formats. Working within an established brand, I focused on creating communication that felt both engaging and accessible, balancing creativity with structure. The role gave me a strong foundation in shaping cohesive visual identities and adapting them across multiple channels.
